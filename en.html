About Me

Why I do this work

My path started with a question, not with code: why do some things work, and others don't?

I studied project management engineering because I learned that anything — from a building to a brand — falls apart without real structure. I went on to study personality psychology because I wanted to understand what people actually respond to, not what we assume they want.

Along the way, I wrote and published a book of poetry and prose with a philosophical outlook. Not for show — because I learned an idea you don't follow all the way through never becomes real.

I bring these three together: engineering structure, understanding people, an eye for beauty. The result is work that's beautiful and one of a kind.
